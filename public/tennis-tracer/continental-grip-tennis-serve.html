Continental tennis grip: identify and check your hand position

A continental grip is a specific hand position on the racket handle, not a grip size or an overgrip product. Use the handle bevels and the position of your index-finger base knuckle as reference points when checking it against the illustrated source.

Compare the same hand and handle view

Right-handed and left-handed diagrams must be interpreted for the hand holding the racket. Match the orientation before copying a reference; a mirrored photo can make the position confusing.

Separate identification from shot selection

Identifying a grip does not establish that it is the right choice for every stroke. Consult the source’s stroke-specific explanation and, for technique corrections, a qualified coach. A ball-trail effect cannot identify your hand position.

Use this checklist

  • Identify which hand the source diagram shows.
  • Take a close view of the handle and base knuckle.
  • Compare hand position before reviewing a full-speed stroke.
