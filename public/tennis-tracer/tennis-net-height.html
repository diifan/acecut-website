Tennis net height at the centre and posts

A regulation tennis net is 3 feet (0.914 metres) high at the centre and 3½ feet (1.07 metres) at the posts. The centre strap holds the middle of the net at its specified height.

Measure the centre separately

Checking a post does not establish the height at the centre. Measure at the centre strap and then check the support arrangement specified for the court.

What a recording cannot establish

Camera height and perspective change the apparent clearance over the net. Use a physical measurement for setup; use video to review visible play, not to certify net height.

Use this checklist

  • Locate the centre strap.
  • Measure the centre height directly.
  • Check the applicable singles or doubles support arrangement.
