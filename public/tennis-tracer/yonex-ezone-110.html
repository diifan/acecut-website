Read these figures consistently

Head size: 110 in²

The area of the racket head. Keep square-inch and square-centimetre figures separate from linear measurements.

Length: 27.25 in

Overall racket length. Compare the same unit and confirm whether you are looking at a standard or extended variant.

Strung weight: 9.6 oz / 272 g

Mass with strings installed in the cited specification. This is not interchangeable with an unstrung figure.

Swingweight: 302

A rotational measurement reported by the source. It is a different property from static mass; keep the measurement method consistent when comparing.

Stiffness rating: 66

A stiffness rating as reported by this source, not an AceCut test result or a medical comfort claim.

Beam width: 26mm / 29mm / 24mm

The reported beam thickness. Multiple values describe different positions rather than separate models.

String pattern: 16 Mains / 18 Crosses

The count of main and cross strings. Confirm that it belongs to this exact model variant.

Before comparing another racket

  1. Match the model name, variant and any edition year shown in the source. Similar names can have different specifications.
  2. Match the measurement condition. Do not compare a strung weight with an unstrung weight or treat swingweight as static weight.
  3. Record your own setup separately, including strings and added accessories. A listed specification is not a measurement of your individual racket.

Source and limitations

Read the specification reference for Yonex EZONE 110. Where the reference omits a property, this page leaves it out. Prices, stock, manufacturer changes and subjective review ratings are not inferred from the table.
