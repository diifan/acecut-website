Playing a tennis pusher: build a point-review plan

Start by separating the opponent’s style from your own errors. Review whether points ended through your missed attacking shot, a short ball you did not use, or a passing shot after your approach. Choose a tactical experiment based on that pattern rather than trying to hit every ball harder.

Make a small point log

For several points, record the first short ball, your next decision and how the point ended. These are observations from your match, not a promise that one tactic defeats every defensive opponent.

Test one decision at a time

Choose one pattern to discuss with your coach, then compare a few points where you used it with points where you did not. Keep the opponent’s court position in view; a close crop of your racket alone loses the tactical context.

Use this checklist

  • Log the point-ending error or winner.
  • Mark whether a short ball appeared before the point ended.
  • Choose one tactical decision to compare in the next session.
