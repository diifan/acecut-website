A source-led tennis video review workflow for new balance ct rally 2.

Start with the tennis context

Read the topic reference from www.tennis-warehouse.com alongside your own recording. The source was checked on 2026-11-26. This short excerpt identifies the context for the review:

for a clay court surface, the New Balance CT Rally shoes struck a nearly perfect blend of outsole grip versus slip on every hard…

The workflow below focuses on what you can observe in footage you own or have permission to edit. It does not claim that AceCut has analysed the source page or any player mentioned there.

Review one continuous rally

  1. Choose a single uninterrupted point.
  2. Mark the visible start and end of the ball flight you want to show.
  3. Split the clip at camera cuts or replays.
  4. Watch the finished segment at normal speed and remove any trail that jumps away from the ball.

Make your clip plan

Use this worksheet to state what you will look for before uploading a segment. It stays in your browser; it does not upload footage.

Apply a trail, then check it

Upload your own video to AceCut’s online tennis Tracer and choose a segment of up to 30 seconds. Select one of the 25 available visual styles. Processing may take several minutes; blurred, hidden or very small balls can produce missed or inaccurate detections.

Play the result at normal speed. The trail should follow visible ball points. If it jumps, bridges a camera cut or obscures the shot, shorten the segment or keep the plain clip. A trail is an editing effect, not a measured spin rate, ball speed or official line call.
