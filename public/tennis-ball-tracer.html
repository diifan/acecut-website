Tennis Ball Tracer: Add a Ball Trail to Your Highlights

Help viewers follow the shot you want them to see.

What is a tennis ball tracer?

A tennis ball tracer adds a visible trail to the ball’s path in a video. The overlay helps draw attention to a shot’s arc and placement, especially when a viewer is watching a short clip on a small screen. In AceCut, it is an editing option for your tennis highlights.

Choose a rally where the trail adds context

A serve, a cross-court exchange or a shot that changes direction can be a useful place to try a tracer. First watch the original clip and decide which moment you want the viewer to follow. Keep enough of the rally to show why the shot matters instead of relying on the trail to tell the whole story.

Add the tracer as part of your edit

Import your tennis footage into AceCut, browse the rallies and select the clips you want to keep. Adjust their start and end, then choose whether to include the ball tracer before exporting. You can save one moment or use multiple rallies in an AI highlight reel. See the highlight editing guide for the full workflow.

Review the result at normal speed

Watch the finished clip from beginning to end. Check whether the trail helps you follow the ball and whether it distracts from either player or the shot’s landing. If an overlay does not make a particular clip clearer, export that edit without it. Clear source footage remains useful even when a trail is added.

Start with a clear recording

Use a fixed camera and keep the court visible. Motion blur, an obstructed view or a ball that is very small in the frame can make the source video harder to follow. Review a test rally before filming a match; the recording guide explains what to check.

A visual effect, not an official line call

AceCut focuses on rally clips, ace moments, highlights and ball tracer. A visible trail in an edited video is not an official line-calling or match-officiating system. Use it to share the feeling of a shot; do not treat the overlay as proof that a disputed ball was in or out.
